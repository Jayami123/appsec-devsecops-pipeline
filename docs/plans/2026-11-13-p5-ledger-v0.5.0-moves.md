### D-LED-B3: schema extension for two statuses

- context: the schema had no case status for a confirmed bug, and its private entries allowed only `held-private`. The brief
  needs TOK-001 as a case, and the two cross-tenant rows as private entries carrying their confirmed status.
- options considered: (a) keep `gated` or `planned` for TOK-001 (untrue); (b) drop the confirmed status from the private rows
  (loses information the brief asks for); (c) add the status to the case enum and the private enum, and update the runtime check.
- choice: (c). `cases[].status` gains `confirmed-product-bug`. `private[].status` accepts `held-private` or
  `confirmed-product-bug`. `src/security/findings.ts` gains `PRIVATE_STATUSES`, and `isPrivateEntry` accepts only those values
  (redaction still fails closed on any other status).
- tests: `tests/unit/docs/ledger.test.ts` (counts, the status set, the confirmed-case and cross-tenant private tests),
  `tests/unit/security/findings.test.ts` (accept and reject cases), `tests/unit/kit/ci/report-redaction.test.ts` (the R11 error
  text now names the allowed statuses).
- evidence: `docs/findings/ledger.schema.json`; `src/security/findings.ts`; the test files above.
